Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Version 0.1

LicenseLogger is operated by UltraForge LLC (“we,” “us”), a New Jersey limited liability company. This policy covers the website at licenselogger.com, the sample desk at app.licenselogger.com, and the LicenseLogger student phone app when it is released.

Short version: We collect driving hours and location so a student, a parent, and a school can share one record. We do not sell personal data or use location for advertising. The live desk today is a sample with no accounts.

1. Who this covers

Schools (owners, front desk, instructors), paying customers (a parent/guardian or an adult student), and student drivers. The product is used by teens age 13–17 with a parent or guardian as the customer login. We do not offer seats to children under 13.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and school records when Auth exists: name, email, role, school, student roster, package and invoice fields.
  • Student profile on the phone: name, birthday, state, optional permit date, optional parent contact and school code. Stored on the device first.
  • Location and motion when a drive is started or auto-track is on: GPS points to measure miles and draw a route, and on iOS Motion & Fitness to tell driving from sitting still. Location is not collected when tracking is off.
  • Hours and lessons: start/end, practice vs instruction, night flag, sparse route, school bookings, checklist progress.
  • Site usage: the marketing site is static. We do not run an advertising pixel on licenselogger.com.

3. How we use it

To compute permit hours (including the 15 mph honesty rule), show the state checklist, run the school Board and Plan, show the parent portal, and provide support. We do not sell this data. We do not use precise location for ads.

4. Sharing

Inside a school, the people with a seat see the students they are allowed to see (owner/admin vs instructor vs parent). We may use infrastructure vendors (hosting, email, future Auth) as processors. We disclose if the law requires it.

5. Retention and deletion

Phone hours stay on the device until the student joins a school or deletes the app data. When accounts exist, a school or parent can ask us to delete a student record at support.ultraforgellc@gmail.com.

6. Sample desk

app.licenselogger.com currently shows fictional sample students. Do not enter real names or real minors’ data there.
