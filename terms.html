2. What the hours mean

Practice credit is the lesser of elapsed time and distance at 15 miles per hour. Trips under 0.5 miles may be discarded. Night hours follow the window the school or profile sets (default 6pm–6am). State hour goals and the test-ready checklist are a compiled snapshot and can lag a DMV change. Confirm every requirement with the state DMV before a road test. The in-app hours report is not a DMV filing.
