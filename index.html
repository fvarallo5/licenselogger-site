    Questions

    Is the live desk a real school?

    No. app.licenselogger.com is a sample week for Green Light Driving (Austin) so you can click Board, Plan, Students, and the portal. There is no account and no live student data.

    Does this replace a DMV log?

    It is a driving-hours record for the family and the school. It is not a DMV filing and not a certificate of completion. Confirm your state’s form with the DMV.

    How do hours count?

    Credit is the lesser of elapsed clock time and distance at 15 miles per hour. Trips under 0.5 miles are discarded. Night uses a 6pm–6am window unless the school sets another.

    Who is this for if the student is 16?

    The student uses the phone. The parent or guardian is the customer login. Age rating and privacy terms cover 13–17 student data. Under 13 is not a seat.

    Is there an App Store listing?

    The store name LicenseLogger is reserved. The student app is not in TestFlight for the public yet. Schools can preview the desk today and write us for a demo.
